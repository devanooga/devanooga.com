## devanooga hacknights

Our `#hacknight` is an informal coworking session. Bring whatever you&rsquo;re working on, hop on voice/screenshare, and get unstuck (or just hang out). It&rsquo;s currently held over **Discord**; ask in [Slack](/slack/)&rsquo;s `#hacknight` channel for the current invite link.

We&rsquo;re actively looking for an in-person space in the Chattanooga area. If you have a venue suggestion, please [reach out](/contact/).

## Where else to plug in

Chattanooga and the surrounding region have a lot going on outside of devanooga. A few places worth following:

- The `#events` channel in our [Slack](/slack/), where members regularly share meetups, conferences, and other gatherings.
- [Open Chattanooga](https://www.codeforamerica.org/brigades/open-chattanooga), the local Code for America brigade.
- Local user groups for specific stacks. Check our [Slack channels](/channels/) for stack-specific chatter (`#dotnet`, `#ham`, `#homelab`, etc.).

## Running an event?

If you&rsquo;re organizing a Chattanooga-area tech event and want the community to know about it, post in `#events` on [Slack](/slack/) or [contact us](/contact/) and we can help amplify.
