## Who we are

devanooga is a community of developers, designers, sysadmins, hardware tinkerers, technology educators, and the people who work alongside them, based in and around Chattanooga, Tennessee.

We&rsquo;re a registered **501(c)(3) nonprofit**. Donations to devanooga are tax-deductible in the United States.

## What we do

- **[Slack](/slack/)** is our primary chat. It&rsquo;s where conversations happen day-to-day, where job opportunities are posted in `#opportunities`, and where beginners ask questions in `#truebeginners`.
- **[Forums](/forums/)** for longer-form discussion that doesn&rsquo;t fit in a Slack channel.
- **Discord** for our `#hacknight` voice and screenshare sessions. Ask in Slack&rsquo;s `#hacknight` channel for the current invite.
- **[Hacknights](/events/)** are informal coworking/build sessions, currently online. We&rsquo;re always looking for an in-person Chattanooga space if you know of one.
- **[Member projects](/projects/)** showcase things our members build.
- **[Outreach](/outreach/)** lists members who offer discounted or free services to local nonprofits and charities.
- **[Radio Freqs](/radio-freqs/)** is our amateur radio sub-club (callsign W3DEV).

## Our values

devanooga is a member of the [Electronic Frontier Alliance](https://www.eff.org/EFA-FAQ). We endorse free expression, security, privacy, creativity, and open access to knowledge.

We expect everyone in our spaces to follow our [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct/). The short version: treat others the way you would want to be treated.
